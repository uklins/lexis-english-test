LEXIS provides a rapid, accurate picture of your current English level across the skills tested in CET-4/6, TOEFL iBT, IELTS, and SAT — so your preparation targets the right areas from the start. It is designed for post-Gaokao and current university students preparing for high-stakes exams or a study-abroad pathway.

The test covers four sections: Vocabulary, Grammar, Reading, and Writing. Each section runs two modules: your Module 1 score (5 questions) determines whether Module 2 presents harder or easier material, calibrating the difficulty to your level in real time. The Writing section is structured differently — its Module 2 is a ten-minute timed essay, graded by AI rather than MCQ.

LEXIS is developed by Leachoy under Project Dawnbreak, an independent nonprofit study initiative. For study resources, video guides, and updates, visit Leachoy's Bilibili channel.

Feedback and suggestions are welcome — reach us at leachoy.rsx@outlook.com or via Bilibili DM.
